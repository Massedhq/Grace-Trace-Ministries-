Grace Trace Career Ready Program

Required for All Residents

As part of your housing placement with Grace Trace Ministries, all residents participate in the Career Ready Program. This program begins preparing you for employment and life after release before you ever arrive. Your participation is not optional — it is a core part of the Grace Trace experience and is designed to give you every advantage possible from day one.

Resume development
Career assessments
Interview preparation
Employer introductions
Workforce training
Education planning
Transportation planning
Life skills preparation
I understand that participation in the Grace Trace Career Ready Program is required as part of my housing placement with Grace Trace Ministries. I agree to actively participate in the program before and after my arrival.

Registration Received

Thank you for registering with Grace Trace Ministries. Your information has been received and our team will reach out to you within 2 business days to discuss next steps.

What Happens Next

  • Our intake team will review your registration and confirm your eligibility.
  • You will be contacted at the phone number or email you provided to schedule an intake conversation.
  • We will begin coordinating services based on your needs — including transportation, documents, and employment planning — before your release date.
  • You will receive information about the Grace Trace Career Ready Program and what to expect upon arrival.
