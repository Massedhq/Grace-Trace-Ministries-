Substance Use Recovery & Treatment Coordination

Grace Trace Ministries connects participants with qualified, licensed treatment professionals who provide individualized alcohol and substance use assessments, counseling, treatment planning, and ongoing recovery support.

Through our network of community treatment partners, participants may receive:
  • Substance use screenings and professional assessments
  • Outpatient or residential treatment referrals
  • Individual and group counseling
  • Relapse-prevention education
  • Peer recovery and accountability support
  • Medication-assisted treatment referrals, when appropriate
  • Coordination with probation, parole, courts, and reentry agencies
  • Continued case management and transitional housing support
Treatment services are provided by independent, appropriately licensed partner organizations. Grace Trace Ministries coordinates referrals, housing support, and continuity of care.
