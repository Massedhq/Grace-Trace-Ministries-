Government Agencies

Trusted Housing & Reentry Partner

Grace Trace Ministries partners with correctional agencies, veterans' programs, and government organizations by providing structured transitional housing, accountability, case management, workforce development, and individualized support. Our goal is to help agencies place eligible individuals into safe, professionally managed environments that promote stability and successful community reintegration.

Referral Partners

A Reliable Community Referral Resource

We welcome referrals from parole officers, probation departments, courts, hospitals, behavioral health providers, social workers, nonprofit organizations, veteran service organizations, and community agencies. Every referral receives a professional intake review, individualized service planning, and access to structured housing and supportive services designed to encourage long-term success.

Donors & Community Partners

Invest in Lasting Change

Your partnership helps provide more than housing — it helps create opportunities for individuals rebuilding their lives. Donations and community partnerships support safe residential environments, employment readiness, transportation assistance, life-skills education, and case management that empowers residents to become self-sufficient and productive members of their communities.

Residents & Families

A Safe Place to Rebuild

Whether you are returning home after incarceration, transitioning from homelessness, a veteran seeking stable housing, or a young adult working toward independence, Grace Trace Ministries provides a structured environment focused on safety, accountability, dignity, and personal growth. Our team works alongside every resident to develop an individualized path toward permanent housing and long-term independence.
