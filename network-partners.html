Our Network Partners

Grace Trace Ministries is committed to building a strong network of partners who share our dedication to housing stability, community reintegration, and long-term independence for the individuals we serve.

Partnership Opportunities
Who We Are Looking to Partner With

We believe that lasting change happens through collaboration. Grace Trace Ministries actively seeks partnerships with organizations across multiple sectors that share our commitment to serving returning citizens, veterans, individuals with disabilities, and young adults on their path to independence.

🏛️

Government Agencies

We seek partnerships with correctional agencies, veterans' programs, courts, and government organizations that place eligible individuals into structured transitional housing. Our programs are designed to meet the compliance, reporting, and accountability standards required by government partners.

⚖️

Referral Organizations

We welcome referral relationships with parole and probation departments, courts, hospitals, behavioral health providers, social workers, and nonprofit organizations looking for a reliable structured housing placement for their clients.

💼

Workforce & Employment Partners

We seek partnerships with employers, staffing agencies, and workforce development organizations committed to providing fair employment opportunities for individuals completing our transitional housing programs.

🏥

Healthcare & Behavioral Health Providers

We look to partner with healthcare providers, mental health organizations, and substance abuse treatment centers to ensure our residents have access to the medical and behavioral health support they need throughout their program journey.

🎓

Educational Institutions

We seek relationships with community colleges, vocational schools, adult education programs, and GED preparation organizations to connect residents with educational opportunities that support long-term self-sufficiency.

🤝

Community & Faith-Based Organizations

We welcome partnerships with faith-based organizations, nonprofits, food banks, legal aid providers, and community groups who share our mission of supporting individuals as they rebuild their lives with dignity and purpose.

🏢

Corporate & Business Partners

We seek corporate sponsors, second chance employers, and business partners willing to invest in our mission through financial support, in-kind donations, volunteer engagement, and employment opportunities for our program graduates.

💛

Donors & Philanthropic Partners

We welcome relationships with individual donors, private foundations, and philanthropic organizations whose giving priorities align with housing stability, reentry support, veterans services, and community development across Texas.
